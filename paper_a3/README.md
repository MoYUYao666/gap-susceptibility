| manuscript | `draft/main.pdf`, 13 pages, 0 errors, 0 undefined citations/references |

Headline findings: human captions win at every site on RSICD and NWPU-Captions but lose on RSITMD and DIOR-RSVG whose
human text is not a whole-image caption; on NWPU-Captions the metadata arm reaches 0.189 at its 31,500-pair ceiling,
above the human arm's 0.126 at 10k pairs; text-tower adaptation lowers the modality gap while joint adaptation usually
gives the best retrieval; seed standard deviation of R@1 is 0.0043 on average.
