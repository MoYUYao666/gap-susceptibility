## 1. LaTeX-ready paragraph (supplement)

> **Draft sentence, ready to paste.** The critical rank is not a single number, and the archive says so in two independent ways. Inside LLaVA-1.5-7B, the ten-seed refit places the sign change of $\Delta E$ at $r_c=5.334$ (95\% paired-seed bootstrap CI $[5.195417, 5.467364]$), bracketed by $r=5$ and $r=6$ (`rc_by_family.llava-1.5-7b.strata.llava_blank3ep_cloud10.crossing_point`, `.crossing_bracket`, `.bootstrap.ci95`); the five-seed re-run on the current script defaults gives $5.260$ inside the same bracket (`.strata.llava_rerun5.crossing_point`). Outside that one protocol the crossing moves, from $4.96$ on the ShareGPT stratum to $46.76$ on the one-epoch stratum (`rc_by_family.llava-1.5-7b.sensitivity_span`), so what the data support is a bracketed crossing under a named protocol, not a model constant. Across backbones it is weaker still: of the 5 families examined, 3 contain a crossing in at least one stratum, 2 contain none anywhere in their measured rank range (\texttt{clip-vit-base-patch32} and \texttt{qwen2-vl-7b}), and only 1 yields a single well-defined crossing (`conclusion.families_with_a_crossing_in_at_least_one_stratum`, `.families_with_no_crossing_anywhere`, `.families_with_one_well_defined_rc`). On Qwen2-VL-2B the sign of $\Delta E$ is set by the image condition rather than by the rank — in the training-energy field every rank $r=1..16$ is positive with real images and negative with blank ones (`rc_by_family.qwen2-vl-2b.strata.qwen2b_p3d_real_train.sign_of_all_means`, `.strata.qwen2b_p3d_blank_train.sign_of_all_means`) — and the CLIP ViT-B/32 retrieval arm likewise shows no zero crossing over $r=1..16$ in any of its four strata, its sign being set by the training window and by which energy is read rather than by the rank (`rc_by_family.clip-vit-base-patch32.strata.*.sign_of_all_means`). We therefore restrict $r_c\in[5,6]$ to the LLaVA configuration and protocol under which it was measured. On the adaptive baseline, the archived comparison does not reverse on the energy axis: in all 4 cells where AdaLoRA and the $\Gamma$-guided rule share budget, learning rate and seed, AdaLoRA reaches the lower alignment energy (per-cell differences $[-3416.5625, -2789.873047, -4059.398438, -4242.140625]$, mean $-3627.0$, 95\% $t$ CI $[-4678.51181, -2575.475495]$; `adalora_boundary.matched_cells[*].difference_adalora_minus_gamma`, `.summary.mean_difference`, `.summary.ci95_t`). That comparison is under-powered — with $n=4$ cells the exact two-sided sign test bottoms out at $p=0.125$ — and it is confounded, because the AdaLoRA arm runs the uniform rank-4 plan on all 324 modules, the same rank plan as our plain uniform control (`adalora_boundary.comparison_plane.adalora_and_uniform_share_one_rank_plan`). In the neighbouring comparison the ordering does reverse: plain uniform rank beats the $\Gamma$-guided rule in 3 archived cells, $\eta=3\times10^{-4}$ at seeds 42 and 123; $\eta=10^{-3}$ at seed 123 (`adalora_boundary.reversal_regime.cells`, `.reversal_regime.regime`). Against the published non-adaptive allocation rules at the shared base-rank-1 arm the $\Gamma$ arm is the lowest of the set (`adalora_boundary.published_baseline_rules.rows[*].delta_E`).

## 4. Caveats that must survive editing

1. **Family coverage.** 5 backbones were searched and all 5 have a rank curve. 3 contain a crossing in at least one stratum, 2 contain none anywhere in the measured range (`clip-vit-base-patch32` and `qwen2-vl-7b`) so for them $r_c$ is neither localised nor bounded, and only 1 yields a single well-defined crossing (`conclusion.families_with_a_crossing_in_at_least_one_stratum`, `.families_with_no_crossing_anywhere`, `.families_with_one_well_defined_rc`). One family's label is not stored inside its archive: `qwen2-vl-7b` is inherited from the manuscript phase table (`rc_by_family.qwen2-vl-7b.strata.qwen_cloud_sweep.model_label_source`, `.model_label_confidence`).
2. **Seed counts.** The primary LLaVA stratum has 10 paired seeds; the re-run stratum 5; the E2 batches 3; the sample-window stratum 4 unit (the training window) at a single seed; SmolVLM 3. The five-seed environment stratum is archived as aggregates only and carries no resampling interval (`rc_by_family.llava-1.5-7b.strata.llava_seed5_aggregate.bootstrap.reason`).
3. **Environment.** The software stack accounts for 0.45 of the $r=5$ variance (95\% CI $[0.21198180448528658, 0.7471951281145658]$), and the three archived stack means at $r=5$ are `{"torch 2.12/cu130": -43.7, "torch 2.4/cu121": 25.25, "torch 2.9/cu130": -23.0}` — one of them positive (`stability.environment_share_of_r5_variance`). Any cross-family sentence about $r_c$ must carry this.
4. **Two manuscript statements disagree with the archives and should be reconciled, not copied.** (a) The `tab:sharegpt` caption states the sign flip occurs between $r=5$ and $r=6$, while its own archived means are $r=4:-60.0$, $r=5:2.666667$, $r=6:133.5$ — a zero crossing between $r=4$ and $r=5$, fitted at $4.957447$ (`rc_by_family.llava-1.5-7b.strata.llava_sharegpt.mean_dE_by_rank`, `.crossing_point`). (b) The `fig:ers` caption calls the one-epoch flip *earlier* than the three-epoch bracket, while the archived one-epoch crossing sits at $46.759029$, far above it (`...strata.llava_ers_1epoch.crossing_point`).
5. **AdaLoRA: what is and is not matched.** Matched: budget, learning rate, seed, reference data ($E0 = 23470.8$). Not matched: the block configuration relative to the $\Gamma$ plan, and the cross-entropy axis — the two scripts do not start from the same first-epoch loss, so no matched-loss statement is available (`adalora_boundary.confounds`). The AdaLoRA arm also sits 0.0179\% above the budget under measurement, so budget matching holds in the cost model only (`adalora_boundary.summary` and `.comparison_plane.adalora_config`).
6. **Under-powered, and reported as such.** $n=4$ matched cells give an exact two-sided sign-test floor of $p=0.125$; the direction is unanimous but cannot reach significance at this cell count (`adalora_boundary.summary.exact_sign_test_two_sided_p`, `.exact_sign_test_reaches_p05`).
7. **Not sourced, therefore not asserted.** (i) Whether peft applied the orthogonality regulariser configured as `orth_reg_weight=0.5` in `tools/_p451_baseline_adalora.py`: no archive records it. (ii) The seed labels of the ShareGPT and one-epoch strata, which the archives store as positional lists. (iii) A $\Gamma$ arm trained at the same base-rank-1 configuration is available, but the three published-rule arms are not parameter-matched to the uniform $r=5$/$r=6$ arms, which the manuscript already calls a directional comparison.
8. **Metric comparability.** The CLIP stratum's energy is a normalised-embedding retrieval gap on a different scale from the VLM alignment energy, so only the *sign* of its $\Delta E$ is comparable across families (`rc_by_family.clip-vit-base-patch32.strata.clip_n16_test.metric_comparable_across_families`). Its four strata are sign-constant along the rank axis: training energy is $+0.016663$ at 16 pairs and $-0.034967$ at 1024, held-out energy is $-0.009098$ and $-0.001448$ (`...strata.clip_n16_train.mean_dE_by_rank`, `.clip_n1024_train`, `.clip_n16_test`, `.clip_n1024_test`).
9. **One stratum reports more than one crossing, and it is the ShareGPT one.** Its curve crosses zero three times (up between $r=4$ and $r=5$ at $4.957$, down between $r=32$ and $r=48$, up again between $r=48$ and $r=64$ at $50.292$); the primary entry is the first forward crossing and the others are kept in `rc_by_family.llava-1.5-7b.strata.llava_sharegpt.forward_crossings` and `.reverse_crossings`. A 'critical rank' read off this stratum alone would be ill-defined, which is part of why the family has no single $r_c$.

## 5. Independent check on the same runs

The refit reproduces the archived per-rank means of the ten-seed family exactly (`stability.archived_exact_tests_on_the_same_runs.refit_reproduces_archived_means`), and the already-archived exact test on those same runs separates 14 of 15 rank pairs after Holm--Bonferroni (`...n_separating_holm_p05`). The curvature observable, which is rank-indexed on the same backbone, does not corroborate a rank threshold: along its primary seed line the second-order share is negative at every archived rank in $[5, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 64]$ and rises monotonically toward zero without crossing it (`stability.curvature_observable.ranks_measured`, `.primary_seed_curve_share_by_rank`); its rescaled share at the operating rate reproduces the manuscript's `tab:hessian` values to four decimals (`...manuscript_tab_hessian_check.reproduces_manuscript_to_4dp`). The sign is not unanimous across seeds and probe steps (1 archived sign flip, `.significant_flips`), which is what the manuscript's own claim table already records.

## 6. One-line answers to the two objections

- *The critical rank was taken from another family.* It was not taken from another backbone — its archive is LLaVA-1.5-7B throughout — but it does belong to one protocol: inside LLaVA the stratum crossings run from $4.96$ to $46.76$, 2 of the 5 backbones contain no crossing anywhere in their measured range, and none of the LLaVA strata is a cross-environment constant.
- *AdaLoRA is a counterexample.* On the energy axis it is not a counterexample but a consistent winner in every matched cell (4 of 4), which the manuscript already concedes; what the boundary table adds is that the win is under-powered ($p\ge0.125$ at this cell count) and confounded with the uniform rank plan, and that the ordering *does* reverse against plain uniform rank in 3 archived cells.
