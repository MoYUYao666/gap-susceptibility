## Cost model correction (2026-10-25, measured on the RTX 4080 SUPER instance)

The estimate recorded earlier in this document - "~25 min per arm, 6 arms = 2.5-3.5 GPU hours" - is **superseded**.
Measured on the instance during the official-implementation comparison:

| quantity | earlier estimate | measured |
|---|---|---|
| training time per arm | ~25 min | **36 s** |
| full process per arm | ~25 min | **55 s** |
| peak VRAM per training arm | 4-8 GB (rule-of-thumb) | **12,923 MiB** |
| whole `--stage train` (5 arms really needed training) | 2.5-3.5 GPU h | **~5 min** |
| GPU utilisation during training | (7 % reading) | **79-100 %** |

Two consequences that change future planning:

1. **The 7 % GPU reading was taken while the card was idle.** The parallelism rule must therefore be applied from a
   measurement taken *during* training, never from an idle snapshot.
2. **The concurrency ceiling on a 32 GB card is 2 for this workload** (2 x 12.9 GiB = 25.8 GiB against the 31.47 GiB
   usable; 3 processes = 37.8 GiB). This was not a guess: a three-process attempt died with
   `torch.OutOfMemoryError ... 31.47 GiB of which 49.94 MiB is free ... 12.6 GiB in use`, and the single arm that
   survived under concurrency took 54 s against 36 s serially (~1.5x slower). For a stage this short the serial run is
   the correct choice, with the OOM logs as the recorded justification.

## Run-to-run dispersion of a single arm (2026-10-25, important for how numbers are quoted)

The same arm (`official_corrected_map`, seed 42, byte-identical argv) produced **delta_E = +250.5 / +181.0 / +182.5**
across three runs, with an identical `E(0) = 3926.0` and identical first-epoch step losses to four decimals. The
cause is inside training: `paper3/scripts/run_training.py` sets `torch.manual_seed` but not
`use_deterministic_algorithms` / `cudnn.deterministic`, and 4-bit bitsandbytes dequantisation plus cuBLAS reductions
are not bit-reproducible.

A dedicated replicate pass then measured the matrix-level spread: official seeds 42/123/789 gave 186.0 / 155.0 / 220.0
against the first pass's 182.5 / 178.0 / 242.0, and reimplementation seeds 42/123 gave 265.5 / 88.5 against
333.0 (an older-environment archive) / 186.0 - a typical spread of about **+/- 25** with a worst case near **100**.
Seed 123's paired difference even flipped sign, from -8.0 to +66.5.

**Therefore**: with a pre-declared equivalence margin of 196.05 and an archived official-minus-reimplementation gap of
92.5, a single-seed `delta_E` must not be quoted as a reproducible reading. Any sentence derived from this comparison
has to carry the measured spread, and the replicate archive
(`outputs/analysis/p3_official_igu_replicates.json`) is the evidence for it.

One further incident worth remembering: the forced replicate pass overwrote the pinned S27 anchor
(`verify_r1_s42_bcexplicit_v11_s1_m3_d1.json`, delta_E 333.0 -> 265.5), which made the next `verify_inputs()` fail
with `archived reimplementation arm delta_E +265.5 FAIL ... refusing to run`. The driver's own restoration step put
both anchors back byte-exactly (sha256 `212e9c75...` 937 B and `a060e2a8...` 946 B, independently re-verified and
also frozen under `cloud_retrieved/par_arms_backup/anchors/`). Any future forced retrain must restore the pinned
anchors before `--compare`, and the round notes must say the snapshot drift was intentional and repaired.
