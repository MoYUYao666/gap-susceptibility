To the Handling Editor, Pattern Recognition Letters

Re: PRLETTERS-D-26-02671 — "Gap susceptibility: a dynamical theory of cross-modal alignment under PEFT"

Dear Editor,

We write about our manuscript PRLETTERS-D-26-02671, currently under review, on three matters, and would be grateful for your guidance on whether to upload updated files now or to hold these changes for the revision. No files have been uploaded with this message.

1. One sentence needs correction. In our discussion of the random-allocation comparison, the submitted text states that three of the ten allocations have a lower mean than the Γ-guided arm, and that "the third is lower by 264". Re-checking our archived per-plan results shows that only two plans are below the Γ-guided arm (by 2928 and 1700, as printed), and that the next plan is in fact 264 above it (+1741.35 against +1477.45). The conclusion of that paragraph — that the difference is indistinguishable within noise — is unchanged, and no other result is affected. We would rather correct a known misstatement in the version of record than leave it in the paper.

2. The supplementary material has been extended, and the additions bound rather than extend the claims. The supplement has grown from 8 to 26 pages (S1–S17 to S1–S51). The new analyses quantify claims the paper already makes and make their limits explicit: a seed-paired analysis of the allocation comparison at matched capacity (−3570.10, 95% CI [−5524.16, −1850.31]; all six pairs negative); a family-wise analysis of the rank crossing, which does not support a single r_c across backbones; a robustness analysis of the Γ ordering against its measured estimation noise; a variance decomposition over four software and hardware stacks; a second downstream task (POPE, 3000 questions), in which accuracy and token F1 improve while the yes-class F1 falls, i.e. a metric-dependent outcome; and a comparison against the official implementation of the allocation rule (paired difference −59.5 against a pre-declared margin of 196.05, verdict "small", with a measured run-to-run spread of up to 132 that is of the same order as the difference being judged).

3. Data availability. The submitted version cites archive version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23523263 (v2.2). The archive has since advanced to 10.5281/zenodo.23632347 (v2.5); the v2.2 snapshot remains permanently available, so the submitted version's claims remain checkable exactly as stated.

The main text remains 10 pages, so the journal's length limit is respected; all additions are confined to the supplementary material. Please let us know whether you would prefer that we upload the updated manuscript and supplement now, or hold them until the revision.

Yours sincerely,

Hongrui Wan
on behalf of all authors
hw4c24@soton.ac.uk
